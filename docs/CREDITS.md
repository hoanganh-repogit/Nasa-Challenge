# Credits

Independent educational prototype; not an official NASA product and not endorsed by NASA. No NASA or JPL logo is
used. Images are used under the [JPL Image Use Policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/).

## Data

- **Traverse table** `MERB_traverse_5130`: MER Analyst's Notebook, PDS Geosciences Node, Washington University in
  St. Louis; traverse by Tim Parker. https://an.rsl.wustl.edu/mera/AN/pages/mer/mer_traverse.htm. Not a formally
  archived PDS product.
- **Mission facts:** NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive, Opportunity (2003-032A).
  https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=2003-032A

## 3D model

- **Spirit and Opportunity Rover 3D Model** (`24883_MER_static.glb`), NASA/JPL-Caltech.
  https://science.nasa.gov/resource/spirit-and-opportunity-rover-3d-model/ (compressed from 11.9 MB to 970 KB
  with gltf-transform: meshopt geometry compression, textures resized to 1024 px WebP; all 9 material groups kept).
